Overview

What is AutoFormBench?

AutoFormBench is a benchmark dataset designed to support research in automated form understanding — the task of detecting, localizing, and classifying fillable elements within structured documents such as government applications, healthcare records, and enterprise invoices.

The dataset comprises 407 manually annotated real-world PDF forms spanning three primary domains. All documents were converted to high-resolution images at 300 DPI to preserve fine-grained visual features such as radio buttons, hairline borders, and small-print labels.

Each document was annotated using a custom Python tool that rendered pages as images and allowed annotators to define and classify regions of interest. Ground truth is stored as structured JSON files mapping each field to its bounding coordinates and functional class.

The benchmark was developed as part of a study comparing classical OpenCV approaches against four YOLO architectures (YOLOv8, YOLOv11, YOLOv26-s, YOLOv26-l). YOLOv11 consistently outperforms all other approaches, achieving the strongest balance of precision and recall across all three element classes. Evaluation uses a tolerance-based matching protocol at 5%, 10%, and 20% of image dimensions — reflecting the practical requirements of downstream form-filling automation.
